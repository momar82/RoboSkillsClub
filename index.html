Studica Robotics Kit Specifications

Industrial Autonomous Mobile Robotics Hardware

WorldSkills Skill 20 equips competitors with enterprise-grade robotics hardware engineered for high-precision autonomous navigation and manipulation.

Studica VMX-pi Controller

Integrated Raspberry Pi 4 Model B running Ubuntu & ROS 2, paired with a real-time FPGA coprocessor and factory-calibrated 9-DOF navX-sensor IMU. Features Flex DIO (8–11), High-Current DIO (12–21), and 12-bit Analog ADC (22–25).

Titan Quad Motor Driver

4-channel H-bridge driver delivering up to 20A per channel at 15.6 kHz PWM frequency. Supports 4 optical quadrature encoders, active CAN bus communication (ID 42), onboard voltage/current monitoring, and fused power outputs.

Multi-Modal Sensor Suite

360° 2D LiDAR scanner for SLAM, SR Pro camera for AprilTag/marker recognition, Sharp GP2Y IR sensors (10–80 cm), HC-SR04 ultrasonic sonar (2–400 cm), analog contrast light sensors, and dual-layer physical/digital E-Stop safety.
