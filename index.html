Engineering Pedagogy

Why RoboSkillsClub?

A rigorous, physical-first learning methodology designed for autonomous robotics excellence. We do not stop at simulation—students construct, calibrate, and program physical machines.

1. Mechanical Rigidity & Fastener Safety

High-frequency motor vibrations shake standard nuts loose in 10 minutes. Students learn structural engineering using 6061-T6 aluminum U-channels, diagonal caliper squaring within ±0.5 mm, and vibration-proof Nyloc locknuts. A software algorithm cannot compensate for a loose motor mount!

2. Distributed Dual-Brain Architecture

Why bottleneck a small onboard robot computer with 3D point cloud graphics? Our architecture divides duties: high-level SLAM mapping, RViz2 3D views, and PyQt5 telemetry run on an Ubuntu student workstation, linked over Gigabit Ethernet (< 0.5 ms latency) to the onboard real-time FPGA and Titan motor controllers.

3. Industrial Sensor Fusion & CAN Bus

Hands-on integration with 360° 2D planar LiDAR laser scanners, Orbbec 3D RGB-D depth perception, factory-calibrated navX 9-axis IMU yaw tracking, and 1 Mbps CAN bus motor telemetry (ID 42) reading high-resolution optical quadrature encoders.

4. Real-Time ROS 2 Autonomy & SLAM

From gamepad joy teleoperation and velocity commands to CycloneDDS multicast network isolation, costmap 2D obstacle inflation, and SLAM mapping. Students progress from raw hardware drivers to complete autonomous mission navigation.

Industry Leading Hardware

Studica Robotics Architecture

Studica Robotics provides the industry-leading hardware platform for hands-on Autonomous Mobile Robotics education and research.

Studica VMX-pi Controller

Integrated Raspberry Pi 4 Model B running Ubuntu Linux and ROS 2, paired with a real-time FPGA coprocessor and factory-calibrated 9-DOF navX-sensor IMU. Features Flex DIO (8–11), High-Current DIO (12–21), and 12-bit Analog ADC (22–25).

Titan Quad Motor Driver

4-channel H-bridge driver delivering up to 20A per channel at 15.6 kHz PWM frequency. Supports 4 optical quadrature encoders, active CAN bus communication (ID 42), onboard voltage/current monitoring, and fused power outputs.

Multi-Modal Perception Suite

360° 2D planar LiDAR for SLAM navigation, Orbbec 3D RGB-D depth camera for spatial mapping, Sharp GP2Y IR sensors (10–80 cm), optical motor encoders, and dual-layer safety interlocks.

The 5-Step Student Engineering Journey

Every student starts with raw aluminum channels and graduates with a fully autonomous robot capable of LiDAR SLAM.

1
Chassis & Nyloc Mechanics Assemble rigid aluminum frame; verify diagonal squareness within 0.5 mm tolerance.
2
Dual-Brain Commissioning Set static Gigabit network, exchange SSH keys, and configure CycloneDDS multicast.
3
Titan CAN Bus Telemetry Drive motors via CAN ID 42, monitor real-time battery voltage, and calibrate encoders.
4
LiDAR & 3D Vision Stream 360° planar laser scans and process depth point clouds for obstacle avoidance.
5
Telemetry Dashboard Execute precision courses, evaluate telemetry, and earn your verified certificate.
